\opening{Dear Members of the Fellowship Committee,}

I am writing to apply for the Predoctoral Fellowship Fund for Spring 2027. I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Statistics, jointly supervised by Professors HaiYing Wang and Jun Yan. My current support for Fall 2026 totals 20 hours and includes teaching supervised by Professor Chi (hosting three discussion sessions) and research supervised by Professor Yan.

My major research activities include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item My dissertation research focuses on rare-feature-adaptive subsampling for regression models. The resulting first-authored manuscript is under review.
  \item As a research assistant, I completed a collaborative project with Servier Pharmaceuticals on predictive-modeling-assisted interim decisions for adaptive trials with censored survival outcomes. The resulting manuscript is under revision.
  \item I developed the R package \texttt{subsampling}, available on CRAN, for optimal subsampling in scalable statistical modeling. It includes the newly developed rare-feature methodology from my dissertation and supports generalized linear models and quantile regression models.
  \item I was a co-first author of an under-review survey of random subsampling that synthesizes inferential targets, estimators, and sampling procedures.
\end{itemize}

With research funding from my advisors and our department, I have presented research over the past three years through invited talks at JSM, NESS, and ICSA and posters at NESS and NERDS. I am grateful for this support; my work has received the ICSA Applied Statistics Symposium Student Paper Award, the NESS Poster Award, and the NERDS Student Poster Award.

My long-term goal is a research-focused academic career in statistics, developing methodology in scalable modeling, survival analysis, and clinical-trial design. The fellowship would help me advance toward this goal by giving me greater flexibility to focus on my dissertation and produce additional research outputs.
