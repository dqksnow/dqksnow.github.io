\begin{reslist}[1pt]

  \item \textbf{Research Assistant} (Statistical Methodology) \hfill Aug 2023 -- Present \\
  University of Connecticut
  \begin{bulletlist}[0pt]
    \item \textbf{Primary output:} First-authored methodology paper on rare-feature-adaptive subsampling for regression models; manuscript under review.
    \item Developed theory showing that estimation efficiency for rare binary covariate coefficients is driven by the limited number of rare observations, explaining numerical instability and slow convergence.
    \item Introduced a rarity-adaptive subsampling method that quantifies feature rarity to ensure adequate representation of rare features, improving robustness and efficiency.
    \item Implemented the methods in the subsampling R package; validated performance via theoretical results, simulation studies, and real-data applications; produced documentation and reproducible examples.
