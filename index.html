Research Interests

Subsampling methods for scalable statistical modeling

I study principled subsampling strategies that preserve statistical efficiency while reducing computational cost. This includes work on generalized linear models, quantile regression, rare events, and rare binary features.

Survival analysis and model averaging

My background includes accelerated failure time models, additive hazards models, and frequentist model averaging methods for censored outcomes.

Methodology that stays close to real data problems

I enjoy projects where statistical ideas meet implementation, whether that means clinical trial design, health data analysis, or domain-specific exploratory work.

My Research Projects

Software

R package subsampling

University of Connecticut

I developed methods and software for optimal subsampling in large-scale statistical models. The package supports generalized linear models, softmax regression, rare event logistic regression, and quantile regression.

Academia-Industry Collaboration

Adaptive clinical trial design with Servier

Research Assistant • Mar 2024 - Present

This project studies how machine learning can improve interim analysis for censored survival outcomes, including the prediction of event times for censored individuals and more accurate conditional power estimation.

Health Data

SDOH, frailty, and accelerated aging in breast cancer patients

Research Assistant • Jul 2024 - Aug 2024

I worked on data preprocessing and analysis using the All of Us platform to study associations among social determinants of health, frailty index, and accelerated aging.

Cross-Disciplinary Project

Sensor-response analysis for nitroaromatic compounds

Research Project • Mar 2024 - Present

I applied clustering and statistical analysis to fluorescence responses of porphyrinoid sensors to improve detection and differentiation of explosives.
